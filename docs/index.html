KubeKubeDashDash

A desktop dashboard for Kubernetes — multi-window, multi-tab, reactive.

Built with Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Desktop · Connects via kubeconfig contexts

Download View on GitHub

Cluster overview

See your cluster's health at a glance — CPU and memory usage, node count, running pods, and a live event stream all on one screen.

Light & dark themes

Pick dark, light, or follow the system — every screen, panel, and chart adapts.

Many windows, many tabs

Each cluster gets its own tab. Drag a tab between windows to reorganize, or tear one off into a new window — the connection moves with it.

All clusters at a glance

Open more than one cluster and an extra tab appears — aggregated CPU, memory, and pod usage across every connected cluster, a card per cluster, and one merged event feed.

Topology

See your cluster as a live graph — workloads, pods, services, and the relationships between them. Cards show status at a glance and the view refreshes on a configurable interval.

Take action on your cluster

KubeKubeDashDash goes beyond read-only browsing. Evict or force-delete pods, cordon and drain nodes, scale and rollout-restart workloads, trigger or suspend CronJobs, and approve or deny certificate signing requests — each from the resource's detail panel, every destructive step behind a confirmation dialog.

Detail panels that go deep

Many kinds get resource-specific detail tabs. Roles and RoleBindings resolve their effective rules and subjects, ResourceQuotas show live usage bars, and EndpointSlices link straight through to the Service that backs them.

Custom resources

CRDs are discovered automatically when you connect and listed in a dedicated Custom Resources sidebar section — searchable, grouped by API group, and browsable just like built-in kinds.

Command palette

Press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K) to jump anywhere — fuzzy-search across every resource kind, namespace, and open tab, then navigate with the keyboard alone.

Live logs

Stream pod logs in a dockable bottom drawer without leaving the dashboard. Open multiple log tabs at once; each is scoped to its cluster and keeps streaming as you navigate.

Workloads

Browse every workload across all namespaces, inspect replica counts and pod statuses, and drill into individual resources for full details.

Also available:

  • StatefulSets
  • DaemonSets
  • ReplicaSets
  • Jobs
  • CronJobs

Cluster & nodes

Check node conditions and allocatable resources, navigate namespaces, and watch cluster events as they happen.

Config & secrets

Inspect ConfigMaps and Secrets across all namespaces and browse their keys and values without leaving the dashboard.

Networking

Map out your cluster's network layer — browse Services, Ingresses, Endpoints, and Network Policies in a consistent interface.

Also available:

  • Ingresses
  • IngressClasses
  • Endpoints
  • EndpointSlices
  • Network Policies

Storage

Track the lifecycle of your persistent volumes and claims — see capacity, access modes, and binding status across storage classes.

Also available:

  • CSIDrivers

Access control & governance

Audit who can do what and keep the cluster within its guardrails — full RBAC, autoscaling and disruption budgets, governance limits, and admission webhooks, all browsable in the same consistent interface.

Access control (RBAC):

  • ServiceAccounts
  • Roles
  • ClusterRoles
  • RoleBindings
  • ClusterRoleBindings
  • CertificateSigningRequests

Autoscaling & disruption:

  • HorizontalPodAutoscalers
  • PodDisruptionBudgets

Governance:

  • ResourceQuotas
  • LimitRanges
  • PriorityClasses

Admission:

  • ValidatingWebhookConfigurations
  • MutatingWebhookConfigurations

Settings

Configure refresh intervals, theme, and kubeconfig context — all from a single settings panel without restarting the dashboard.
